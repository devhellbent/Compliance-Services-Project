FSSAI Registration

Overview

FSSAI Registration is a mandatory legal compliance for all Food Business Operators (FBOs) in India, overseen by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI). The FSSAI, established under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, is the apex body responsible for protecting and promoting public health through the regulation and supervision of food safety. This registration or license ensures that all food products—from manufacturing to distribution—undergo quality checks and adhere to strict hygiene standards.

Types of FSSAI Registration in India
- FSSAI Basic Registration: Mandatory for small-scale businesses with an annual turnover of up to ₹12 Lakhs. This includes petty retailers, hawkers, and small-scale cottage industries.
- FSSAI State License: Required for medium-sized FBOs with an annual turnover between ₹12 Lakhs and ₹20 Crores. It covers mid-scale manufacturers, distributors, and restaurants operating within a single state.
- FSSAI Central License: Mandatory for large-scale FBOs with an annual turnover exceeding ₹20 Crores (or ₹30 Crores for wholesalers). It is also required for importers, exporters, and businesses operating at major ports, airports, or in multiple states.

Features of FSSAI Registration

- 14-Digit Registration Number: A unique identifier provided upon successful registration, which must be displayed on business premises and food packaging.
- Legal Compliance: Ensures adherence to the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, avoiding penalties and legal action.
- Validity Period: The registration is valid for 1 to 5 years, as chosen by the FBO, and must be renewed before expiry.
- Consumer Trust: Signals a commitment to food safety and hygiene standards, building consumer confidence.
- FoSCoS Portal: The application process is primarily online through the Food Safety Compliance System (FoSCoS) portal.
- Tiered Structure: Classification into Basic, State, or Central based on turnover, production capacity, and nature of the business.

Advantages and Disadvantages of FSSAI Registration

Advantages
- Legal Stamp of Approval: Grants formal authorization to operate a food business in India.
- Enhanced Credibility: Assures customers about the safety and quality of the food products.
- Market Reach and Expansion: Mandatory for listing on online food platforms (like Swiggy, Zomato) and participating in government tenders.
- Brand Building: Distinguishes the business from unlicensed competitors and enhances brand reputation.
- Access to Funding: Makes the business eligible for government schemes, subsidies, and easier bank loans.
- Standardized Procedures: Encourages the adoption of a Food Safety Management System (FSMS) for better hygiene.

Disadvantages
- Compliance Costs: Involves upfront and recurring fees for application and mandatory periodic renewals.
- Administrative Complexity: Detailed documentation and rigorous scrutiny are required, especially for State and Central licenses.
- Strict Regulatory Standards: Continuous effort and resources are needed to maintain mandated safety and quality standards.
- Risk of Penalties: Non-compliance can lead to heavy fines, product recalls, or license suspension.
- Mandatory Upgrade: Requires conversion from Basic to State or Central licenses if turnover exceeds prescribed limits.

Eligibility Criteria for FSSAI Registration

Every individual or business involved in manufacturing, processing, packaging, storing, distributing, or selling food products must obtain FSSAI registration.

Key Eligible Categories:
- Food Manufacturers: Producers of snacks, dairy, sweets, bakery items, oils, and beverages.
- Food Traders and Retailers: Grocery stores, supermarkets, and wholesalers.
- Food Storage and Warehouse Operators: Facilities for storing perishable or non-perishable food.
- Restaurants and Food Outlets: Hotels, cafes, canteens, and cloud kitchens.
- Food Distributors and Transporters: Logistics providers moving food products.
- Importers and Exporters: Entities involved in the international trade of food items.
- Petty Food Business Operators: Small-scale units like hawkers, temporary stallholders, and home-based food businesses.

Documents Required for FSSAI Registration

For Basic Registration (Form A)
- Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, or Voter ID of the proprietor/partners.
- Address Proof of Premises: Electricity bill, rent agreement, or property deed.
- Photograph: Recent passport-size photo of the applicant.
- Declaration: Signed declaration form confirming compliance with safety standards.

For State and Central License (Form B)
- Entity Proof: Certificate of Incorporation, Partnership Deed, or LLP Agreement.
- Infrastructure Details: Blueprint/layout plan of the processing unit showing dimensions and operation-wise area allocation.
- List of Directors/Partners: Names and addresses with identity proof.
- Equipment Details: List of machinery and equipment used (with capacity and horsepower).
- Product Information: List of food categories and products to be manufactured or handled.
- Water Test Report: Report from a recognized lab confirming water quality (if used as an ingredient).
- Source of Raw Material: Procurement plan for milk, meat, or other raw materials.
- Import Export Code (IEC): Mandatory for importers and exporters.

Fees

Fees and Penalties of FSSAI Registration in India

FSSAI Registration and License Fees (Per Annum)
- Basic Registration: ₹100 (Turnover up to ₹12 Lakhs).
- State License: ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 (Turnover between ₹12 Lakhs and ₹20 Crores).
- Central License: ₹7,500 (Turnover above ₹20 Crores or for importers/exporters).

Main Penalties for Non-Compliance
- Operating Without License: Fine up to ₹5 Lakhs and/or imprisonment up to 6 months.
- Substandard Food: Fine up to ₹5 Lakhs.
- Misbranded Food: Fine up to ₹3 Lakhs.
- Misleading Advertisement: Fine up to ₹10 Lakhs.
- Unhygienic Processing: Fine up to ₹1 Lakh.
- Unsafe Food (causing injury/death): Fine up to ₹10 Lakhs and/or imprisonment (6 months to life).
- Petty Offenses: Fine up to ₹25,000.
