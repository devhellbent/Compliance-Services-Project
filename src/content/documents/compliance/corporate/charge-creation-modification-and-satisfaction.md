## Extract from MCA — Company Compliance (Private company event filings)

Private Company Compliance

OVERVIEW

Private Company Compliance refers to the mandatory set of legal and regulatory obligations that every Private Limited Company (Pvt. Ltd.) in India must fulfill to remain active and operational under the Companies Act, 2013, and associated rules.

These requirements are continuous and are broadly categorized into annual compliance and event-based compliance. Failing to meet these deadlines leads to heavy monetary penalties and potential director disqualification.

Here is a breakdown of the core compliance requirements:

Annual Compliance (The Yearly Cycle)

These tasks must be performed every financial year (April 1st to March 31st).

Board Meetings: The company is required to hold at least four Board Meetings in a calendar year, ensuring that the gap between any two consecutive meetings does not exceed 120 days. Proper minutes must be maintained for each meeting.

Annual General Meeting (AGM): An AGM must be conducted once a year by the shareholders to adopt the audited financial statements, appoint auditors, and transact other ordinary business. This meeting must be held on or before September 30th following the end of the financial year.

Statutory Audit: All Private Limited Companies must appoint a Statutory Auditor who is a practicing Chartered Accountant (CA). The auditor examines the financial records and issues an Audit Report, which is then submitted to the government. The appointment must be intimated to the Registrar of Companies (RoC) using Form ADT-1 within 15 days of the AGM.

Filing of Financial Statements: The audited Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss Account, and Director's Report must be prepared and filed with the RoC using Form AOC-4. The due date for this is 30 days from the date of the AGM.

Filing of Annual Return: Details of the company's structure, including shareholding pattern, directors, and changes during the year, must be filed with the RoC using Form MGT-7/MGT-7A. The due date is 60 days from the date of the AGM.

Director KYC: Every individual holding a Director Identification Number (DIN) must file an annual KYC with the MCA using Form DIR-3 KYC by September 30th.

Income Tax Compliance: The company must file its Corporate Income Tax Return using Form ITR-6 by September 30th (if a tax audit is not applicable).

Event-Based Compliance

These filings are triggered only when a specific change or event occurs in the company:

Change in Leadership: Any appointment, resignation, or change in the details of a Director or Key Managerial Personnel requires filing Form DIR-12.

Share Allotment: Whenever a company issues or allots new shares to an existing or new shareholder, Form PAS-3 must be filed with the RoC.

Charge Creation/Modification: If the company obtains a loan from a bank or financial institution that is secured by company assets (creating a charge), this charge must be registered with the RoC using Form CHG-1 or CHG-9.

Special Resolutions: Certain important decisions made by the shareholders, such as altering the company's Memorandum of Association (MoA) or Articles of Association (AoA), must be filed with the RoC using Form MGT-14 within 30 days of passing the resolution.

Other Regulatory Compliance

In addition to ROC filings, a private company must comply with various other laws:

GST: If the company is registered under the Goods and Services Tax (GST) regime, it must ensure timely filing of monthly, quarterly, or annual GST returns.

TDS/TCS: Compliance with Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) and Tax Collected at Source (TCS) rules, including timely deposits and filing of quarterly returns.

Labour Laws: Obligations under the Employees’ Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, ESI Act, and Professional Tax laws, based on the company's employee count and location.
